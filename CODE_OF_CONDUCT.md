# Code of conduct

## What this covers, and what it does not

This document is about how people treat **each other** in this project's spaces:
issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else the project gathers.

It is **not** a content policy for what you write with Evermind. Evermind
deliberately ships without moderation, and the stories people tell with it are
their own business. Nothing here reaches into your conversations, your
characters, or the model you chose. Those two things live in different rooms and
this document only governs one of them.

## The standard

Be decent to the people you are talking to. Concretely:

- Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith until they clearly
  are not. Most disagreements here are two people holding different pieces of
  the context.
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This breaks when the context window is
  full" is useful. "This is amateur nonsense" is not, and it is also less
  informative.
- Take a no gracefully. A maintainer declining your contribution is not a
  judgement of you, and a contributor declining to rewrite their patch is not an
  insult either.
- Respect that people here have different first languages, timezones, hardware
  and levels of experience. Someone writing clumsy English is doing you the
  courtesy of speaking yours.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, sustained hostility, or following someone across threads to keep
  at them
- Attacks on who a person is rather than what they wrote: their race, gender,
  sexuality, religion, nationality, disability, age or appearance
- Sexual attention directed at another participant. Writing adult fiction with
  this software is the point of it; aiming that at a real person in an issue
  thread is not the same activity and will not be treated as one.
- Publishing someone's private information
- Deliberately derailing threads, or reopening a settled decision until everyone
  gives up

## Reporting

Report privately through
[GitHub's private advisory form](https://github.com/Phobetore/Evermind/security/advisories/new).
It is the repository's private inbox and reaches the maintainer without anything
becoming public first. It says "security" on the tin; use it for this too.

Tell me what happened and where. You will get an acknowledgement, and I will not
discuss your report with anyone other than the person it concerns, and only as
far as resolving it requires.

## What happens next

I decide, and I say so plainly. Depending on what happened, that means a private
word, a public correction, edited or removed comments, or a block from the
repository. Sustained or deliberate harassment goes straight to the last one.

This is a small project with one maintainer, so there is no committee and no
appeals process. What there is instead is a commitment to explain any action
taken to the person it affects.
